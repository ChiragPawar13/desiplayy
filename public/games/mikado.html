How to Play Mikado

Interactive Camera

Drag your left mouse button (or swipe on touch screen) to rotate the camera. Use zoom or right-click drag to pan. Look carefully from different angles to spot overlapping sticks!

Picking a Stick

Simply click or tap a stick to pick it up. If there are other sticks resting on top of it, they will shake/disturb and your pick will fail.

Rules of Turn

In Solo Mode, any failed pick scores a "strike". 3 strikes and the game ends. In Pass & Play or Bot modes, failing a pick transfers the turn instantly to the opponent, while a successful pick awards points and grants you another turn.

Game End

The game finishes when all 31 sticks are picked. The player with the highest total points wins!
